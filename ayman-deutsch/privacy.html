يحفظ التطبيق على جهازك فقط: الدروس التي أنهيتها، وحجم الخط الذي اخترته. تُحذف هذه المعلومات عند حذف التطبيق أو مسح بياناته.

Privacy Policy

"Learn German with Ayman" (com.ayman.deutsch) does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data. The app has no internet permission and works fully offline. Only your lesson progress and font size are saved locally on your device. German words are read aloud by your phone's own text-to-speech engine. There are no ads and no third-party analytics.
